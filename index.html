The Kortemme Lab recently demonstrated that a de novo protein can be designed to occupy two distinct conformations rather than one static fold. I built a compact structural explorer around I89, an engineered calcium-binding protein from that study, to ask where its experimentally solved apo states diverge and how that motion rewires residue contacts.

The analysis compares the representative NMR models for apo I89 state 1 (PDB 9CIG) and state 2 (PDB 9CID). It is descriptive rather than a dynamics simulation: two experimentally observed structures are aligned, then read as snapshots of the conformational landscape.

How it works

I extracted the 90 shared Cα atoms, aligned state 1 to state 2 with a Kabsch fit, and measured the displacement of each residue. I also built a coarse contact map using pairs closer than 8 Å, excluding neighbors fewer than four residues apart in sequence. The two views connect global movement to local packing changes.

A focused structural lever: residues 68-74 are only 7 of the 90 analyzed positions, yet they average a 6.58 Å displacement and participate in 19 of 44 state-specific contact changes. That makes this segment a compact place to connect design, biochemical validation, and higher-throughput mutational testing.

Why this matters for the lab

The specialist role connects computational protein design with yeast display, protein expression, purification, and biochemical assays. This explorer frames an experimentally tractable loop: prioritize positions from structural displacement and contact rewiring, design a focused mutation panel, then test how expression, stability, ligand response, and state preference move together.

What I would bring

I would bring careful wet-lab execution from vaccine R&D and analytical characterization, plus computational experience with Python, AlphaFold 3, ProteinMPNN, and biological data analysis. I am especially interested in helping carry designed sequences through reproducible protein-production and assay workflows, then using the resulting data to sharpen the next design round.

Methods and limitations

This is a reanalysis of public structures, not a claim about an unobserved mechanism. Model 1 from each NMR ensemble was used, and all shared Cα atoms were included in the global alignment. The 8 Å contact threshold is a coarse structural summary; atom-level hydrogen bonds, side-chain packing, ensemble uncertainty, calcium binding, and transition kinetics require dedicated analyses and experiments.
